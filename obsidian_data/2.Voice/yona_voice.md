# Yona Voice: 요나체 템플릿 가이드

지금까지 네가 학생들에게 쓴 메시지들을 보면 꽤 일관된 "요나체"가 있어.

단순히 친절한 영어가 아니라, **따뜻하지만 담백하고, 감정은 진심인데 과하지 않고, 학생을 존중하는 느낌**이 강해.

내가 패턴을 분석해보면 특징이 이래.

* "Thank you"를 정말 자주 사용한다.
* 상대를 칭찬할 때 결과보다 **노력**을 칭찬한다.
* "I hope"보다 "I’m happy", "I appreciate", "I’d love"를 많이 사용한다.
* 명령형을 거의 쓰지 않는다.
* 항상 학생에게 선택권을 준다.
* 너무 비즈니스적으로 끝내지 않고 인간적인 한 문장을 마지막에 넣는다.

예를 들면

> Thank you for studying so sincerely.
>
> I always enjoy our lessons because of you.

이런 문장들.

그래서 아래처럼 템플릿을 만들어두면 거의 모든 학생에게 조금씩 수정해서 사용할 수 있어.

---

## 1. 수업 끝나고

Hi ○○ 😊

Thank you so much for studying hard today.

I know today's lesson wasn't easy, but you did a really great job.
Please take some time to review today's notes, and don't worry if you can't remember everything right away.

Little by little, you're making real progress.

See you in our next lesson.
Have a wonderful day 🤍

---

## 2. 숙제 칭찬

Thank you for taking the time to do your homework.

I can really see how much effort you're putting into your Korean, and it makes me so happy as your teacher.

Keep going little by little.
You're doing much better than you think. 😊

---

## 3. 학생이 미안하다고 할 때

No worries at all. 🤍

Thank you for letting me know.

I completely understand that life gets busy sometimes.
Please take good care of yourself first.

We'll continue whenever you're ready.

See you soon 😊

---

## 4. 학생이 힘들다고 할 때

Thank you for sharing that with me.

I'm really sorry to hear you've been having a difficult time.

Please don't put too much pressure on yourself.
We'll go at your pace.

I hope things get a little easier for you soon. 🤍

---

## 5. 가격 이야기

Thank you for sharing your thoughts with me.

I really appreciate your honesty.

Unfortunately, I'm not able to continue lessons at the previous rate.

That said, I'd be happy to see if we can find something that works for both of us.

I truly enjoy teaching you and hope we can continue learning together. 😊

---

## 6. 여행 가기 전에

Hi everyone 🤍

Just a little update!

I'll be traveling for a while, so my lesson schedule may look a little different than usual.

Thank you so much for your understanding.

I'm really looking forward to seeing everyone again in class soon.

Take care and keep enjoying your Korean journey!

---

## 7. 리뷰 답변

Thank you so much for your beautiful review.

Reading your words truly made my day.

It's been such a joy watching your Korean improve little by little.

Thank you for trusting me and studying with me.

I can't wait for many more fun lessons together. 🤍

---

## 8. 학생이 그만둘 때

Thank you so much for letting me know.

Of course I'll miss our lessons, but I completely understand.

Thank you for all the time and effort you've put into learning Korean with me.

If life becomes a little less busy one day, I'll always be happy to welcome you back.

Take good care of yourself.
Wishing you all the best 🤍

---

## 9. 처음 만나는 학생

Hi ○○ 😊

Thank you so much for your lovely message.

I'm really excited to meet you.

Don't worry at all about your Korean level.
We'll start from wherever you are and build your confidence step by step.

I'm looking forward to meeting you in our trial lesson!

---

## 10. 요나체의 시그니처 마무리

네가 거의 항상 쓰는 느낌을 살리면 마지막은 이런 문장들이야.

* Thank you for always studying so sincerely.
* I always enjoy our lessons because of you.
* Little by little, we'll get there together.
* I'm so happy to be part of your Korean journey.
* Watching your Korean improve is one of my biggest joys as a teacher.
* Thank you for making time for Korean in your busy life.
* Please take good care of yourself.
* Looking forward to seeing you soon.
* Have a lovely day. 🤍
* With love, Yona 🌸

---

### 내가 느낀 "요나체"를 한 문장으로 정의하면

> **"학생을 가르치는 선생님"이라기보다, 학생의 한국어 여정을 함께 걷는 사람처럼 말한다.**

그래서 네 메시지는 "You need to..."보다 **"We'll..."**, "Don't worry...", "Little by little..." 같은 표현이 자연스럽게 많고, 학생들이 리뷰에서 반복해서 말하는 것처럼 **편안하고, 응원받는 느낌**을 주는 게 가장 큰 강점이야.
