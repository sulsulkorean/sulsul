Ordering coffee in Korean can be easy if you know a few key phrases. Start with "아메리카노 주세요" (*Americano juseyo*) to ask for an Americano. This phrase is your go-to for ordering any drink by simply replacing "아메리카노" with your desired beverage. Practice these phrases to order confidently at any Korean cafe.

> - "아메리카노 주세요" means "Please give me an Americano."
> - "큰 사이즈로 주세요" asks for a large size.
> - "테이크아웃으로 주세요" means "Please make it to go."
> - "설탕 없이 주세요" requests no sugar.
> - Use "얼음 많이 주세요" for extra ice.

## What do you actually say at a Korean cafe counter?

When you step up to the counter, start with a polite greeting like "안녕하세요" (*Annyeonghaseyo*). Then, say the drink you want followed by "주세요" (*juseyo*), which means "please give me." For example, "카페라떼 주세요" (*Café latte juseyo*) for a café latte.

**카페라떼 주세요** — *Café latte juseyo*
"Please give me a café latte."
Literal: Café latte please give
Use it when: Ordering a café latte

## How to specify your drink preferences

If you have specific preferences, such as size or sweetness, add those details after the main phrase. For a large size, say "큰 사이즈로 주세요" (*Keun saijeuro juseyo*). To request no sugar, use "설탕 없이 주세요" (*Seoltang eopsi juseyo*).

**큰 사이즈로 주세요** — *Keun saijeuro juseyo*
"Please make it a large size."
Literal: Large size please give
Use it when: You want your drink in a large size

## How to order a drink to go

When you want your coffee to go, use the phrase "테이크아웃으로 주세요" (*Teikeuauteuro juseyo*). This lets the barista know you need a takeaway cup.

**테이크아웃으로 주세요** — *Teikeuauteuro juseyo*
"Please make it to go."
Literal: Takeout please give
Use it when: You want your drink in a takeaway cup

## Steps to order coffee in Korean

1. Greet the barista with "안녕하세요" (*Annyeonghaseyo*).
2. State your drink choice, e.g., "아메리카노 주세요" (*Americano juseyo*).
3. Add any customizations, like "큰 사이즈로 주세요" (*Keun saijeuro juseyo*).
4. Specify if you want it to go with "테이크아웃으로 주세요" (*Teikeuauteuro juseyo*).
5. Thank the barista with "감사합니다" (*Gamsahamnida*).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the basic phrase for ordering coffee in Korean?

The basic phrase is "아메리카노 주세요" (*Americano juseyo*), which means "Please give me an Americano." You can replace "아메리카노" with the name of any drink you want.

### How do I ask for a different size in Korean?

To ask for a different size, you can say "큰 사이즈로 주세요" (*Keun saijeuro juseyo*) for a large size. This phrase helps you get the exact portion you want.

### What should I say if I want my coffee to go?

You can say "테이크아웃으로 주세요" (*Teikeuauteuro juseyo*) to request your coffee to go. This is helpful when you're in a hurry and need a takeaway.

### How do I specify a drink with no sugar in Korean?

Say "설탕 없이 주세요" (*Seoltang eopsi juseyo*) to order your drink without sugar. This ensures your drink matches your taste preferences.
