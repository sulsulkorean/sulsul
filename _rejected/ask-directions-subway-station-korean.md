To ask for directions to a subway station in Korean, use "지하철역 어디예요?" or "지하철역 어디 있어요?" These phrases will help you navigate the subway system with ease. Practice saying them out loud to ensure locals understand your request.

> - Use "지하철역 어디예요?" to ask for a subway station.
> - "가장 가까운 지하철역 어디예요?" means "Where is the nearest subway station?"
> - Add a line number for specific directions: "1호선 지하철역 어디예요?"

## What Do You Say to Find a Subway Station?

When you're trying to find a subway station in Korea, the simplest question is:

**지하철역 어디예요?** — *Jihacheolyeok eodieyo?*  
"Where is the subway station?"  
Literal: subway station where is  
Use it when: asking for the location of any subway station

If you're looking for the nearest station, you can modify the phrase slightly:

**가장 가까운 지하철역 어디예요?** — *Gajang gakkaun jihacheolyeok eodieyo?*  
"Where is the nearest subway station?"  
Literal: nearest subway station where is  
Use it when: you want the closest station to your location

## Asking for Directions to a Specific Line

Sometimes, you need to find a specific subway line. Here's how to ask:

**1호선 지하철역 어디예요?** — *Ilhoseon jihacheolyeok eodieyo?*  
"Where is the subway station for line 1?"  
Literal: line 1 subway station where is  
Use it when: you need a station on a specific line

You can replace "1호선" with any line number you need.

## Steps to Ask for Directions

1. Approach a local or a station staff member.
2. Say "안녕하세요" (Hello) to greet them politely.
3. Use the phrase "지하철역 어디예요?" for general directions.
4. Specify a line if needed: "1호선 지하철역 어디예요?"
5. Thank them with "감사합니다" (Thank you).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I ask for the nearest subway station in Korean?

You can say "가장 가까운 지하철역 어디예요?" which means "Where is the nearest subway station?"

### What if I can't understand the response?

Politely ask them to repeat by saying "다시 말해 주세요." or use a translation app for assistance.

### How do I ask for directions to a specific subway line?

Use "OO호선 지하철역 어디예요?" replacing "OO" with the line number, to ask "Where is the subway station for line OO?"

### What should I do if I get lost?

Find a landmark or a major road and ask "여기 어디예요?" to get your bearings from locals.
