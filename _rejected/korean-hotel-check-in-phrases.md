To check into a hotel in Korea smoothly, start with "예약했어요" (*yeyakhaesseoyo*) to confirm your reservation. When you want your room key, say "방 키 주세요" (*bang ki juseyo*). If you need help, "도와주세요" (*dowajuseyo*) is handy. These phrases will help you manage the check-in process with ease.

> - "예약했어요" — Use this to confirm your reservation.
> - "방 키 주세요" — Ask for your room key with this phrase.
> - "도와주세요" — A polite way to request assistance.
> - "체크인 시간은 몇 시예요?" — Ask about the check-in time.
> - "아침 식사는 포함되어 있나요?" — Inquire if breakfast is included.

## What Do You Actually Say at a Korean Hotel Counter?

When you arrive at a Korean hotel, you'll need to confirm your reservation. Use the phrase:

**예약했어요** — *yeyakhaesseoyo*  
"I have a reservation."  
Literal: reservation did  
Use it when: confirming a booking at the front desk

If you need to ask for your room key:

**방 키 주세요** — *bang ki juseyo*  
"Please give me the room key."  
Literal: room key please give  
Use it when: requesting your room key from the receptionist

## How to Confirm Your Hotel Reservation in Korean

Confirming your reservation is crucial. Here’s how to do it:

1. Approach the front desk and greet with "안녕하세요" (*annyeonghaseyo*).
2. Say "예약했어요" (*yeyakhaesseoyo*) to let them know you have a reservation.
3. If needed, specify your name by saying "제 이름은 [Your Name]입니다" (*je ireumeun [Your Name] imnida*).
4. Wait for the receptionist to confirm your booking.
5. Thank them with "감사합니다" (*gamsahamnida*).

## Asking for Your Room Key in Korean

Once your reservation is confirmed, you’ll need your room key. Here’s the phrase to use:

**방 키 주세요** — *bang ki juseyo*  
"Please give me the room key."  
Literal: room key please give  
Use it when: requesting your room key

## Inquiring About Hotel Amenities

If you're curious about amenities like breakfast, use:

**아침 식사는 포함되어 있나요?** — *achim siksaneun pohamdoeeonnayo?*  
"Is breakfast included?"  
Literal: morning meal is included?  
Use it when: asking if breakfast is part of your stay

## Korean Phrases for Special Requests at a Hotel

Sometimes, you might have special requests. Here’s how to ask for a late check-out:

**늦게 체크아웃 할 수 있어요?** — *neutge chekeuaut hal su isseoyo?*  
"Can I check out late?"  
Literal: late check-out can do?  
Use it when: requesting a late check-out time

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I ask for my room key in Korean?

You can ask for your room key in Korean by saying "방 키 주세요" — *bang ki juseyo*.

### What if I need to confirm my reservation in Korean?

To confirm your reservation, say "예약 확인하고 싶어요" — *yeyak hwagin hago sipeoyo*.

### How do I ask for assistance in Korean at a hotel?

For assistance, you can say "도와주세요" — *dowajuseyo*, which means "Please help me".

### How can I ask for a late check-out in Korean?

To ask for a late check-out, say "늦게 체크아웃 할 수 있어요?" — *neutge chekeuaut hal su isseoyo?*
