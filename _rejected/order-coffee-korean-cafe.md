To order coffee in a Korean cafe without freezing, confidently say "아메리카노 주세요" (*Americano juseyo*) for an Americano. This phrase combines the coffee name with "주세요" to make a polite request. Practice speaking out loud with SULSUL to build confidence and fluency.

> - "아메리카노 주세요" means "Please give me an Americano."
> - "포장해 주세요" means "Please make it takeaway."
> - "다시 말씀해 주세요" helps when you need something repeated.
> - Use "주세요" to politely request any item.
> - Pronunciation practice is key to being understood.

## What do you actually say at a Korean cafe counter?

When ordering coffee in a Korean cafe, the phrase "아메리카노 주세요" (*Americano juseyo*) is your go-to for an Americano. To order other drinks, replace "아메리카노" with the drink you want. For example:

**카페라떼 주세요** — *Kape latte juseyo*  
"Please give me a cafe latte."  
Literal: Cafe latte please give  
Use it when: Ordering a cafe latte

**콜드브루 주세요** — *Koldeu beuru juseyo*  
"Please give me a cold brew."  
Literal: Cold brew please give  
Use it when: Ordering a cold brew

You: **카페라떼 주세요** — *Kape latte juseyo* — "Please give me a cafe latte."  
Them: **사이즈는 어떻게 하시겠어요?** — *Saijeuneun eotteoke hasigesseoyo?* — "What size would you like?"  
You: **중간 사이즈로 주세요** — *Junggan saijeuro juseyo* — "Medium size, please."

## How to politely request a takeaway coffee in Korean?

If you want your coffee to go, use "포장해 주세요" (*pojanghae juseyo*). This phrase is useful for takeaway orders.

**포장해 주세요** — *Pojanghae juseyo*  
"Please make it takeaway."  
Literal: Wrap please give  
Use it when: Requesting a takeaway order

You: **아메리카노 포장해 주세요** — *Americano pojanghae juseyo* — "Please give me an Americano to go."  
Them: **네, 알겠습니다** — *Ne, algesseumnida* — "Yes, understood."

## What usually goes wrong when ordering coffee?

A common mistake is forgetting to use "주세요" at the end of your request, which might make your order sound less polite. Always attach "주세요" to ensure your request is courteous. Another issue is pronunciation; practice speaking clearly to avoid misunderstandings. If you say "아메리카노" too quickly, it might sound unclear, so slow down and articulate each syllable.

## How to respond if you don't understand the barista?

If you can't understand the barista, say "다시 말씀해 주세요" (*dasi malsseumhae juseyo*). This politely asks them to repeat what they said.

You: **다시 말씀해 주세요** — *Dasi malsseumhae juseyo* — "Please say it again."  
Them: **네, 다시 말씀드릴게요** — *Ne, dasi malsseumdeulilgeyo* — "Yes, I'll say it again."

## How to confirm your order in Korean?

After placing your order, the barista might repeat it back to you. Confirm by saying "네, 맞아요" (*Ne, majayo*), which means "Yes, that's right."

You: **아메리카노 주세요** — *Americano juseyo* — "Please give me an Americano."  
Them: **아메리카노 한 잔 맞으시죠?** — *Americano han jan majeusijyo?* — "An Americano, right?"  
You: **네, 맞아요** — *Ne, majayo* — "Yes, that's right."

## How to customize your coffee order in Korean?

Sometimes you might want to customize your order, such as asking for less sugar or more milk. Use "덜 달게 해 주세요" (*Deol dalge hae juseyo*) for less sugar.

**덜 달게 해 주세요** — *Deol dalge hae juseyo*  
"Please make it less sweet."  
Literal: Less sweet make please give  
Use it when: You want less sugar in your coffee

You: **카페라떼 덜 달게 해 주세요** — *Kape latte deol dalge hae juseyo* — "Please make the cafe latte less sweet."  
Them: **네, 그렇게 해드릴게요** — *Ne, geureoke haedeurilgeyo* — "Yes, I'll do that."

## How to ask for a receipt in Korean?

After completing your order, you might need a receipt. Simply say "영수증 주세요" (*Yeongsujeung juseyo*).

**영수증 주세요** — *Yeongsujeung juseyo*  
"Please give me the receipt."  
Literal: Receipt please give  
Use it when: Requesting a receipt after purchase

You: **영수증 주세요** — *Yeongsujeung juseyo* — "Please give me the receipt."  
Them: **여기 있습니다** — *Yeogi itseumnida* — "Here it is."

## Step-by-step guide to ordering coffee in Korean

1. **Greet the barista**: Start with "안녕하세요" (*Annyeonghaseyo*).
2. **State your order**: Use the drink name + "주세요" (e.g., "아메리카노 주세요").
3. **Specify takeaway if needed**: Add "포장해 주세요" for takeaway.
4. **Customize your order**: Use "덜 달게 해 주세요" for less sweet.
5. **Confirm your order**: Respond with "네, 맞아요" if they repeat your order.
6. **Ask for a receipt**: Say "영수증 주세요" if needed.
7. **Thank the barista**: Finish with "감사합니다" (*Gamsahamnida*).
