Korean hair salons range from walk-in 커트 전문점 (*keoteu jeonmunjeom*, cut-only shops) in basement shopping districts to full 미용실 (*miyongsil*, beauty salons) with tinting and treatments. Nail art cafes are on almost every block in Seoul. A photo on your phone handles 80 percent of the conversation — these phrases close the rest.

> - Show a reference: **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*) — "Please do it like this"
> - Request less length: **조금 더 짧게** (*jogeum deo jjalge*) — "A little shorter"
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*) — "How much is it?"
> - Request a nail design: **이 디자인으로 해 주세요** (*i dijaineuro hae juseyo*) — "Please do this design"
> - Leave with a compliment: **잘 해 주셨어요** (*jal hae jusyeosseoyo*) — "You did a great job"

## What do you say when you walk into a Korean hair salon?

Wait for **어서 오세요** (*eoseo oseyo*, "Welcome") — your cue to say what you want. Open your phone to a reference photo and say **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*), "Please do it like this." Most stylists will study the photo and ask one follow-up question about length.

The most common stylist follow-up is about how much to cut:

| Stylist asks | Your answer |
|---|---|
| 길이는 어떻게 할까요? | 조금 더 짧게 (*jogeum deo jjalge*) — "A little shorter" |
| 많이 자를까요? | 조금만 잘라 주세요 (*jogeumman jalla juseyo*) — "Just a small trim" |
| 앞머리도 자를까요? | 네, 같이 잘라 주세요 (*ne, gachi jalla juseyo*) — "Yes, trim the bangs too" |
| 앞머리는요? | 앞머리는 그대로 두세요 (*ape meorineun geudaero duseyo*) — "Leave the bangs as they are" |

**조금 더 짧게** (*jogeum deo jjalge*) is the single most useful phrase — stylists hear it as calm and clear. Hold two fingers close together to show how much, and the message lands in any shop.

## How do you ask for a specific nail design?

Pull up an image and say **이 디자인으로 해 주세요** (*i dijaineuro hae juseyo*), "Please do this design." The technician will check if the colour is in stock.

You: **이 디자인으로 해 주세요.** — *i dijaineuro hae juseyo.* — "Please do this design."  
Them: **이 색은 없는데 비슷한 색으로 할까요?** — *i saekeun eomneunde bisutan saekeuro halkkayo?* — "We don't have this colour — shall I use a similar one?"  
You: **네, 괜찮아요.** — *ne, gwaenchanayo.* — "Yes, that's fine."

For a plain colour, say **단색으로 해 주세요** (*dansaekeuro hae juseyo*), "Plain colour, please," and point to the shade you want on the colour ring.

## How do you check the price and pay?

Before sitting down, ask **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?" Nail designs vary in cost — intricate art costs more than a plain colour — so knowing the price upfront avoids surprises. If you are not sure the salon takes cards, ask **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do you take card?"

When you leave, say **잘 해 주셨어요, 감사합니다** (*jal hae jusyeosseoyo, gamsahamnida*), "You did a great job, thank you." Stylists in Korea genuinely appreciate hearing it on the way out.
