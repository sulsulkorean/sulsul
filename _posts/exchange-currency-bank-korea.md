To exchange currency at a bank in Korea, walk up to the counter and say **환전하고 싶어요** (*hwanjeonhago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to exchange money." The teller asks which currency and how much, then hands you won — catching those two follow-up questions matters as much as the opening line.

> - Open with **환전하고 싶어요** (*hwanjeonhago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to exchange money," as soon as you reach the counter.
> - Name what you have: **달러 100불이요** (*dalleo baek bul-iyo*), "One hundred US dollars."
> - Ask for smaller bills: **만 원짜리로 주세요** (*man wonjjariro juseyo*), "In ten-thousand-won bills, please."
> - If the reply is too fast, say **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly."

## How do you start the exchange at the counter?

Banks in Korea usually have a numbered ticket system near the entrance. Take a ticket, wait for your number on the screen, then approach the counter when called. The reusable pattern is purpose + **하고 싶어요** (*hago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to…"

You: **안녕하세요, 환전하고 싶어요.** — *annyeonghaseyo, hwanjeonhago sipeoyo.* — "Hello, I'd like to exchange money."  
Them: **어떤 돈을 바꾸시겠어요?** — *eotteon doneul bakkusigesseoyo?* — "Which currency would you like to exchange?"  
You: **달러 100불이요.** — *dalleo baek bul-iyo.* — "One hundred US dollars."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Start the exchange | 환전하고 싶어요 (*hwanjeonhago sipeoyo*) |
| Name your currency | 달러 100불이요 (*dalleo baek bul-iyo*) |
| Ask for smaller bills | 만 원짜리로 주세요 (*man wonjjariro juseyo*) |
| Need a receipt | 영수증 주세요 (*yeongsujeung juseyo*) |
| Ask about the rate | 환율이 어떻게 돼요? (*hwan-yuri eotteoke dwaeyo?*) |

## What if you need an ATM instead of the counter?

Many travelers exchange at the airport or use an ATM after hours. For a cash machine, the pattern shifts to **어디 있어요?** (*eodi isseoyo?*), "Where is…?"

You: **저기요, ATM 어디 있어요?** — *jeogiyo, ATM eodi isseoyo?* — "Excuse me, where is the ATM?"  
Them: **1층 로비에 있어요.** — *ilcheung robie isseoyo.* — "It's in the lobby on the first floor."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

A common mistake is saying **환전** (*hwanjeon*, currency exchange) at an ATM screen that only accepts cards. ATMs with a green **외환** (*oehwan*, foreign exchange) sign handle cash exchange; regular ATMs dispense won from your linked account. If the screen is in Korean only, look for **영어** (*yeongeo*, English) in a corner — most bank machines switch languages with one tap.

## What usually goes wrong at the teller window?

The most frequent freeze point is the amount question. Travelers practice the opening line but stumble when the teller asks **얼마를 바꾸시겠어요?** (*eolmareul bakkusigesseoyo?*), "How much would you like to exchange?" Answer with the foreign amount plus the currency name: **달러 50불이요** (*dalleo osip bul-iyo*), not just a number.

Bring your passport — banks require it for cash exchange. If you forgot, say **여권 가져올게요** (*yeogwon gajyeolgeyo*), "I'll go get my passport," rather than leaving the line without explaining.
