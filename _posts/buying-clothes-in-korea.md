When buying clothes in Korea, start with **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?," and ask if you can try items on with **입어 봐도 돼요?** (*ibeo bwado dwaeyo?*). Add **다른 사이즈 있어요?** (*dareun saijeu isseoyo?*) when you need another size. These three lines cover browsing, fitting, and price without a long speech.

> - Say **그냥 구경하고 있어요** (*geunyang gugyeonghago isseoyo*) if you are only browsing.
> - Ask before stepping into a fitting room — use the try-on question above.
> - Point at the size tag and ask if they have that size.
> - Pay with **카드로 할게요** (*kadeuro halgeyo*), "I'll pay by card."

## How do you ask for help without freezing?

Staff may ask **찾으시는 거 있으세요?** (*chajeusineun geo isseuseyo?*), "Are you looking for something?" If you are browsing, answer **그냥 구경하고 있어요** (*geunyang gugyeonghago isseoyo*). If you want help, name the item: **티셔츠 찾고 있어요** (*tisyeocheu chatgo isseoyo*), "I'm looking for a T-shirt."

You: **안녕하세요. 티셔츠 찾고 있어요.** — *annyeonghaseyo. tisyeocheu chatgo isseoyo.* — "Hello. I'm looking for a T-shirt."  
Them: **어떤 색깔 찾으세요?** — *eotteon saekkkal chajeuseyo?* — "What color are you looking for?"  
You: **검은색 찾고 있어요.** — *geomeunsaek chatgo isseoyo.* — "I'm looking for black."

Keep answers short. One color or item name is enough — staff will pull options faster than if you try a long explanation.

## How do you check size and try something on?

Ask **입어 봐도 돼요?** before you disappear into a fitting room. For size, **다른 사이즈 있어요?** works for any garment. Point at the tag and say **이 사이즈로 있어요?** (*i saijeuro isseoyo?*), "Do you have this in this size?"

| Need | Say this |
|---|---|
| Try it on | 입어 봐도 돼요? (*ibeo bwado dwaeyo?*) |
| Another size | 다른 사이즈 있어요? (*dareun saijeu isseoyo?*) |
| Another color | 다른 색깔 있어요? (*dareun saekkkal isseoyo?*) |
| Price | 이거 얼마예요? (*igeo eolmayeyo?*) |
| Too big / small | 커요 / 작아요 (*keoyo / jagayo*) |

A common mistake is nodding at the first size without checking — say **커요** (*keoyo*), "It's big," or **작아요** (*jagayo*), "It's small," so staff can swap it.

## What do you say at the register?

When you are ready, bring the item to the counter and say **이걸로 할게요** (*igeollo halgeyo*), "I'll take this." For payment, **카드로 할게요** or **현금으로 할게요** (*hyeongeumeuro halgeyo*) is enough.

If you need a bag, ask **봉투 주세요** (*bongtu juseyo*), "A bag, please." Posted prices in clothing shops are usually fixed — ask about a posted sale with **세일이에요?** (*seirieyo?*), "Is this on sale?," rather than inventing a discount request.

## Steps to buy clothes in Korean

1. Greet with **안녕하세요** (*annyeonghaseyo*).
2. Browse, or say **티셔츠 찾고 있어요**.
3. Ask the try-on question before entering the fitting room.
4. Ask for another size if the first one does not fit.
5. Pay with **카드로 할게요**.
