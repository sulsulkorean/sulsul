To order food delivery in Korea, open an app like Baemin or Coupang Eats, pick a restaurant, and confirm your address. In the delivery note field, type **문 앞에 두고 벨 눌러주세요** (*mun ape du-go bel nulleojuseyo*), "Please leave it at the door and ring the bell" — the line drivers see most often.

> - Add **문 앞에 두고 벨 눌러주세요** (*mun ape du-go bel nulleojuseyo*) when you want contactless drop-off.
> - Say **배달 왔어요?** (*baedal wasseoyo?*) on the phone when the driver calls.
> - Ask **몇 분 걸려요?** (*myeot bun geollyeoyo?*) to check how long delivery will take.
> - Request **수저도 넣어 주세요** (*sujeodo neoeo juseyo*), "Please include utensils," if the app has a notes box.

## What do you write in the delivery note?

Korean delivery apps have a free-text box for **요청사항** (*yocheongsahang*, special requests). Keep it short — one polite sentence beats a paragraph.

| Situation | Write this |
|---|---|
| Leave at door | 문 앞에 두고 벨 눌러주세요 (*mun ape du-go bel nulleojuseyo*) |
| Call on arrival | 도착하면 전화해 주세요 (*dochakhamyeon jeonhwahae juseyo*) |
| Include utensils | 수저도 넣어 주세요 (*sujeodo neoeo juseyo*) |
| Drive safely | 조심히 안전하게 오세요 (*josimhi anjeonhage oseyo*) |

**벨 눌러주세요** (*bel nulleojuseyo*) means "please ring the bell" — apartment buildings in Seoul often have no doorbell sound in the hallway, so drivers call instead.

## What happens when the driver calls?

Drivers usually phone when they cannot find your unit or when you ordered to a **배달존** (*baedaljon*, delivery zone) at a park or riverside spot.

You: **네, 지금 내려갈게요.** — *ne, jigeum naeryeogalgeyo.* — "Yes, I'll come down now."  
Them: **1층 로비 앞에 두었어요.** — *ilcheung robi ape dueosseoyo.* — "I left it in front of the first-floor lobby."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

**내려갈게요** (*naeryeogalgeyo*) tells the driver you are on your way down — say it even if you are already in the elevator.

## How do you order delivery without a fixed address?

At Han River parks, set the pin to the nearest delivery zone and meet the driver at the listed spot. Tell friends **한강에서 치킨 배달시킬까요?** (*hangangeseo chikin baedalsikilkayo?*), "Shall we order chicken delivery at the Han River?"

Watch for **최소 주문 금액** (*choeso jumun geumaek*, minimum order amount) on the checkout screen — add a side dish if your cart is short.
