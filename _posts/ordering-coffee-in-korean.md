To order coffee in Korean, name the drink and add **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please give me." **아메리카노 한 잔 주세요** (*amerikano han jan juseyo*) means "One Americano, please." Answer temperature, size, and for-here/to-go with one short phrase each.

> - Name the drink and add **주세요** — **아메리카노 한 잔 주세요** (*amerikano han jan juseyo*), "One Americano, please."
> - Answer temperature with **아이스로 주세요** (*aiseuro juseyo*), "Iced, please," or **따뜻한 걸로 주세요** (*ttatteuthan geollo juseyo*), "Hot, please."
> - Say **가지고 갈게요** (*gajigo galgeyo*) for "to go," or **먹고 갈게요** (*meokgo galgeyo*) for "for here."
> - If you don't understand, say **다시 말씀해 주세요** (*dasi malsseumhae juseyo*), "Please say that again."

## How do you place the first order?

The reusable pattern is drink name + **한 잔 주세요** (*han jan juseyo*). **한 잔** (*han jan*) is the counter for one cup, so **라테 주세요** (*rate juseyo*) works too — the counter just makes the quantity explicit.

If the menu name is unfamiliar, point and say **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "This one, please." Unsure what to try? Ask **뭐가 맛있어요?** (*mwoga masisseoyo?*), "What is good here?" A barista would not normally ask what drink you want again after you've already named one — listen for the next question instead.

## How does the exchange usually go at the counter?

Baristas ask about temperature or size right after you order, not before.

You: **아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요.** — *aiseu amerikano han jan juseyo.* — "One iced Americano, please."  
Them: **어떤 사이즈로 드릴까요?** — *eotteon saijeuro deurilkkayo?* — "Which size would you like?"  
You: **작은 사이즈로 주세요.** — *jageun saijeuro juseyo.* — "A small size, please."

**드릴까요?** (*deurilkkayo?*) politely asks what the barista should prepare for you. Answer with the option plus **으로 주세요** (*euro juseyo*) or **로 주세요** (*ro juseyo*), "please make it that way" — you never need a full new sentence.

## What do you say for here or to go?

The barista may ask **드시고 가세요, 가지고 가세요?** (*deusigo gaseyo, gajigo gaseyo?*), "For here or to go?" Answer with one clear phrase.

| Answer | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Staying | 먹고 갈게요 (*meokgo galgeyo*) |
| Leaving | 가지고 갈게요 (*gajigo galgeyo*) |
| Also "to go" | 테이크아웃 할게요 (*teikeuaut halgeyo*) |
| Extra shot | 샷 추가해 주세요 (*syat chugahae juseyo*) |
| Pay by card | 카드로 할게요 (*kadeuro halgeyo*) |

A common mistake is answering the size question before the temperature question has even been asked — wait for each question and answer only what's asked, one word at a time.

## How do you customize a drink without a complicated sentence?

Ask first, then request the change. **두유로 바꿀 수 있어요?** (*duyuro bakkul su isseoyo?*) means "Can you change it to soy milk?" If they say yes, follow with **두유로 바꿔 주세요** (*duyuro bakkwo juseyo*).

For an allergy, ask **우유가 들어가요?** (*uyuga deureogayo?*), "Does it contain milk?" so staff can check the recipe instead of guessing.
