When a new K-drama episode drops at midnight KST, group chats light up — and the phrases fans actually type still work in Korean even if you watched with subtitles. **이미 봤어요?** (*imi bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch it already?" opens the conversation, and **스포일러 하지 마세요** (*seupailleo haji maseyo*), "No spoilers, please," keeps friends from ruining the twist.

> - Ask if they watched: **이미 봤어요?** (*imi bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch it already?"
> - Block spoilers: **스포일러 하지 마세요** (*seupailleo haji maseyo*), "No spoilers, please."
> - Share hype: **진짜 재미있어요** (*jinjja jaemiisseoyo*), "It is really fun."
> - Tease the cliffhanger: **다음 화 기대돼요** (*daum hwa gidaedwaeyo*), "I am looking forward to the next episode."

## How do you talk about a fresh episode without spoiling?

Fan chats mix English drama titles with Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you are following along.

1. Text your group **이미 봤어요?** as soon as you finish.
2. If you are behind, ask friends to hold spoilers before anyone replies.
3. When someone asks your take, say **진짜 재미있어요** — safe, no plot details.
4. If the ending hits hard, add **다음 화 기대돼요**.
5. Before bed, tell a friend **오늘 밤 못 자겠어요** (*oneul bam mot jagesseoyo*), "I cannot sleep tonight," when the cliffhanger is brutal.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Check if they watched | 이미 봤어요? (*imi bwasseoyo?*) |
| Ask for no spoilers | 스포일러 하지 마세요 (*seupailleo haji maseyo*) |
| Safe reaction | 진짜 재미있어요 (*jinjja jaemiisseoyo*) |
| Hype next week | 다음 화 기대돼요 (*daum hwa gidaedwaeyo*) |
| Late-night shock | 오늘 밤 못 자겠어요 (*oneul bam mot jagesseoyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in drama group chats stay quick and emotional.

You: **이미 봤어요?** — *imi bwasseoyo?* — "Did you watch it already?"  
Them: **응, 방금 봤어!** — *eung, banggeum bwasseo!* — "Yeah, I just watched it!"  
You: **스포일러 하지 마세요.** — *seupailleo haji maseyo.*

If someone drops a hint anyway, **아직 안 봤어요** (*ajik an bwasseoyo*), "I have not watched yet," resets the thread politely.

## What usually goes wrong in drama group chats?

Learners memorize romantic lines from subtitles but never practice **다음 화 기대돼요**, the hype line fans send right after a cliffhanger.

A second mistake is typing long English paragraphs while friends switch to Korean for two seconds — one practiced reaction keeps you in the conversation. For lines you hear inside the show itself, see [common K-drama phrases in conversation](/common-k-drama-phrases-conversation).
