Allkpop and fan community boards have been tracking a shift in how international fans approach Korean 팬사인회 (*paensainhoe*, fan sign events): more fans are arriving with a few Korean lines ready, not just an album to get signed. Idols notice the difference — a short greeting in Korean opens the exchange in a way that silent pointing never can. You do not need to be fluent. One sincere sentence is enough to get a genuine response.

> - Greet the idol: **안녕하세요, 팬이에요!** (*annyeonghaseyo, paen-i-eyo!*), "Hello, I'm a fan!"
> - Ask for a personal message: **메시지도 써 주실 수 있어요?** (*mesijido sseo jusil su isseoyo?*), "Can you write a message too?"
> - Give a compliment: **노래가 정말 좋아요** (*noraega jeongmal joayo*), "I really love the songs."
> - Close the moment: **건강하세요!** (*geongang-haseyo!*), "Stay healthy!"

## What do you say when it is your turn at the signing table?

Fan sign lines move fast — you have about thirty seconds per artist. Walk up, make eye contact, and open with **안녕하세요, 팬이에요!** (*annyeonghaseyo, paen-i-eyo!*). That single phrase establishes you as someone who made an effort, and the response is almost always warmer than a silent hand-over.

Add where you are from to go even further: **저는 [나라]에서 왔어요** (*jeoneun [nara]-eseo wasseoyo*), "I came from [country]." Korean artists find it genuinely interesting when fans travel from far away, and will usually reply directly.

| Moment | What to say |
|---|---|
| First greeting | **안녕하세요, 팬이에요!** (*annyeonghaseyo, paen-i-eyo!*) |
| Share your country | **저는 [나라]에서 왔어요** (*jeoneun [nara]-eseo wasseoyo*) |
| Ask for a written message | **메시지도 써 주실 수 있어요?** (*mesijido sseo jusil su isseoyo?*) |
| Saying goodbye | **건강하세요!** (*geongang-haseyo!*) |

## How do you compliment an idol naturally in Korean?

The most useful compliments are specific. **목소리가 정말 예뻐요** (*moksori-ga jeongmal yeppeoyo*) means "Your voice is really beautiful." **무대가 너무 좋았어요** (*mudae-ga neomu joasseoyo*) means "The performance was so good."

Here is how a short exchange in the signing line often goes:

You: **안녕하세요, 팬이에요! 노래가 정말 좋아요.** — *annyeonghaseyo, paen-i-eyo! noraega jeongmal joayo.* — "Hello, I'm a fan! I really love the songs."  
Them: **어머, 한국말 잘하시네요!** — *eomeo, hangungmal jalhasineyo!* — "Oh, your Korean is great!"  
You: **조금만 알아요. 그래도 좋아해요!** — *jogeumman arayo. geuraedo joahaeyo!* — "I only know a little. But I still love it!"

## Can you ask for a photo or a selfie?

Policies vary by event — some fan signs allow photos at the table, others do not. Check the event rules posted by the organizer before you go. If photos are permitted and you want to ask, say **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "Can we take a photo?" If they agree, say **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*), "Thank you" — the casual warmth of that word lands well in this setting.

## How do you send a fan message in Korean before or after the event?

Fan letters and Weverse messages follow a simple pattern. Open with **항상 응원해요** (*hangsang eung-wonhaeyo*), "I always cheer for you." Close with **앞으로도 잘 부탁드려요** (*apeurodo jal butakdeuryeoyo*), "Please keep going — I will be here."

If you are writing a card to tuck inside your album before the fan sign, these two phrases cover everything you need to say. Korean fans use them exactly this way.
