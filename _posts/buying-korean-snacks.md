When buying Korean snacks, you can say **이거 주세요** — *igeo juseyo* — "Please give me this." This phrase is perfect for pointing at items you want. If you're unsure what to try, ask **추천해 주세요** — *chucheonhae juseyo* — "Please recommend one." These phrases help you confidently navigate Korean convenience stores.

> - **이거 주세요** — *igeo juseyo* — Use this to request an item directly.
> - **추천해 주세요** — *chucheonhae juseyo* — Ask for a recommendation.
> - **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* — Inquire about the price.
> - **이거 있어요?** — *igeo isseoyo?* — Check if they have a specific item.
> - **감사합니다** — *gamsahamnida* — Always thank the staff!

## How to Ask for a Snack Recommendation

In a Korean convenience store, if you're overwhelmed by choices, simply say **추천해 주세요** — *chucheonhae juseyo*. This phrase politely asks the staff to recommend a snack. It’s a friendly way to discover local favorites and try something new without feeling lost.

## What If You Can't Find a Snack?

Sometimes, the snack you crave might not be visible. To check availability, ask **이거 있어요?** — *igeo isseoyo?* — "Do you have this?" This phrase is handy for confirming if a specific snack is in stock, making your shopping experience smoother.

## Understanding the Response

You: **추천해 주세요** — *chucheonhae juseyo* — "Please recommend one."  
Them: **이거 맛있어요** — *igeo masisseoyo* — "This one is delicious."  
You: **그럼 이거 주세요** — *geureom igeo juseyo* — "Then, please give me this."

This exchange helps you handle recommendations and responses smoothly, making you more confident in your choices.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

A common mistake is forgetting to use polite language. Remember to use phrases like **주세요** — *juseyo* — to keep interactions respectful. Politeness is key in Korean culture, especially when speaking to store staff.

## Steps to Buy Snacks in Korean

1. Point at the snack you want.
2. Say **이거 주세요** — *igeo juseyo*.
3. Ask **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* for the price.
4. Listen to the price and confirm your purchase.
5. Thank the staff with **감사합니다** — *gamsahamnida*.
