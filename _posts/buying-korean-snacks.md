When buying Korean snacks, say **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "Please give me this," while pointing. Unsure what to try? Ask **추천해 주세요** (*chucheonhae juseyo*), "Please recommend one." Those two lines get you through most convenience-store counters.

> - **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*) — request an item directly.
> - **추천해 주세요** (*chucheonhae juseyo*) — ask for a recommendation.
> - **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*) — ask the price.
> - **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*) — ask if they have a specific item.

## How do you ask and answer at the counter?

You: **추천해 주세요** — *chucheonhae juseyo* — "Please recommend one."  
Them: **이거 맛있어요** — *igeo masisseoyo* — "This one is delicious."  
You: **그럼 이거 주세요** — *geureom igeo juseyo* — "Then, please give me this."

If the snack is not on the shelf, ask **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*), "Do you have this?" A common mistake is dropping **주세요** (*juseyo*) — keep it so the request stays polite.

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 이거 주세요 (*igeo juseyo*) | Please give me this |
| 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) | How much is it? |
| 이거 있어요? (*igeo isseoyo?*) | Do you have this? |
| 추천해 주세요 (*chucheonhae juseyo*) | Please recommend one |
| 감사합니다 (*gamsahamnida*) | Thank you |

## Steps to buy snacks in Korean

1. Point at the snack you want.
2. Say **이거 주세요**.
3. Ask **얼마예요?** if you need the price.
4. Pay, then thank them with **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*).

If the snack is buried on a lower shelf, point at a photo on your phone and ask **이거 있어요?** again — one clear question beats a long search speech.
