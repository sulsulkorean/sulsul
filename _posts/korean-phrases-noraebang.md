Korean phrases for noraebang (노래방, *noraebang*) — the private karaoke room — are short and predictable. Walk in, say how many people you are, ask for a room, and you are inside within two minutes. Every other conversation is just ordering snacks and adding time.

> - Ask for a room: **방 있어요?** (*bang isseoyo?*), "Do you have a room?"
> - State your group size: **세 명이요.** (*se myeongiyo.*), "Three people."
> - Order more time: **한 시간 더 주세요.** (*han sigan deo juseyo.*), "One more hour, please."
> - Call for more snacks: **과자 더 주세요.** (*gwaja deo juseyo.*), "More snacks, please."

## How do you book a noraebang room at the counter?

Walk up to the counter and lead with the number of people. The staff will tell you how long you have and what is included — you do not need a long sentence.

You: **두 명이요, 방 있어요?** — *du myeongiyo, bang isseoyo?* — "Two people — do you have a room?"  
Them: **네, 한 시간 기본이에요.** — *ne, han sigan gibonieyeo.* — "Yes, one hour is the standard."  
You: **좋아요, 주세요.** — *joayo, juseyo.* — "Great, we'll take it."

**기본** (*gibon*) means "standard" or "base package." You might also hear **두 시간 기본이에요** (*du sigan gibonieyeo*), "Two hours standard" — just confirm the number and hand over your payment.

## What do you order once you are inside?

Most noraebang rooms have a phone or a buzzer on the wall to call the counter. Press it and say what you want.

| What you want | Say this |
|---|---|
| More snacks | 과자 더 주세요. (*gwaja deo juseyo.*) |
| Two cans of beer | 맥주 두 캔 주세요. (*maekju du kaen juseyo.*) |
| Tambourine | 탬버린 주세요. (*taembeorin juseyo.*) |
| Extra microphone | 마이크 하나 더 주세요. (*maikeu hana deo juseyo.*) |

If you are not sure how to use the song machine, press any button and say **이거 어떻게 해요?** (*igeo eotteoke haeyo?*), "How does this work?" Staff will come in and show you — no further Korean required.

## How do you add time before the buzzer goes off?

The timer on the screen counts down and a buzzer sounds when your hour is up. Before it rings, call the counter and add time in one line:

**한 시간 더 주세요.** — *han sigan deo juseyo.* — "One more hour, please."

**더** (*deo*) means "more" and plugs into almost anything: **시간 더** (*sigan deo*) for more time, **맥주 더** (*maekju deo*) for more beer, **과자 더** (*gwaja deo*) for more snacks. Learn the word once and it works everywhere in the room.

## What do you say when you leave?

When your time is up and you are heading out, say **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*), "Thank you," at the counter. If you enjoyed the room and want to come back, add **또 올게요** (*tto olgeyo*), "We'll come again." Staff appreciate it — and it is a useful phrase for any shop or restaurant in Korea, not just noraebang.
