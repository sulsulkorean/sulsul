Korean phrases for Incheon Airport start with one question: **이민국 어디예요?** (*iminguk eodiyeyo?*), "Where is immigration?" Get from the gate through baggage claim and onto the AREX train with five short questions — and you will not need to pull out a translation app once.

> - Find immigration: **이민국 어디예요?** (*iminguk eodiyeyo?*), "Where is immigration?"
> - Nothing to declare: **신고할 것 없어요.** (*singohal geot eopseoyo.*), "I have nothing to declare."
> - Baggage claim: **수하물 찾는 곳 어디예요?** (*suhamul chatneun got eodiyeyo?*), "Where do I collect my luggage?"
> - Airport train: **공항철도 어디예요?** (*gonghang cheoldo eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the airport railway?"

## What does the immigration officer ask?

The officer will ask where you are staying and how long you plan to be in Korea. Both answers are one short phrase.

You: **호텔에 묵어요.** — *hotere mugeoyo.* — "I'm staying at a hotel."  
Them: **며칠 계실 거예요?** — *myeochil gyesil geoyeyo?* — "How many days are you staying?"  
You: **열흘이요.** — *yeolheulliyo.* — "Ten days."

If you miss a question, say **다시 말씀해 주세요** (*dasi malsseumhae juseyo*), "Please say that again." Officers hear it constantly — it does not slow anything down.

## How do you get through customs without stress?

Most visitors walk through the green lane and keep moving. State your situation simply:

**신고할 것 없어요.** — *singohal geot eopseoyo.* — "I have nothing to declare."

If a customs officer stops you, they will say **짐 열어봐도 될까요?** (*jim yeoreobwado doelkkayo?*), "May I check your bag?" The polite reply is **네, 괜찮아요** (*ne, gwaenchanayo*), "Yes, that's fine." Say it calmly — a relaxed answer is always the right one.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Asking for baggage claim | 수하물 찾는 곳 어디예요? (*suhamul chatneun got eodiyeyo?*) |
| Nothing to declare | 신고할 것 없어요. (*singohal geot eopseoyo.*) |
| Transit passenger | 환승이에요. (*hwanseungieyeo.*) |
| Currency exchange desk | 환전 어디서 해요? (*hwanjeon eodiseo haeyo?*) |

## Where do you buy a T-money card and get into Seoul?

After arrivals, look for a convenience store or a kiosk near the exit. Point and ask **티머니 카드 어디서 사요?** (*timeoni kadeu eodiseo sayo?*), "Where do I buy a T-money card?" The card works on every subway, bus, and taxi in Korea — get one before you leave the airport.

For the AREX airport train (**공항철도**, *gonghang cheoldo*), the entrance is one level below arrivals. If you cannot find it, ask any staff member:

**공항철도 타는 곳 어디예요?** — *gonghang cheoldo taneun got eodiyeyo?* — "Where do I board the airport railway?"

They will point you there without you needing another sentence.

## What if you decide to take a taxi instead?

Taxi ranks are outside every arrivals exit, and drivers are used to foreign passengers.
