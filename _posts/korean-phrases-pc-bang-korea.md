PC방 (*PC bang*) are Korea's 24-hour gaming and internet cafes — you pay by the hour, pick a numbered seat on a floor-plan screen, and can order ramen without leaving your chair. Knowing a handful of phrases turns what can feel like a complicated check-in into a two-sentence exchange.

> - Ask for a seat: **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*) — "Is there a seat available?"
> - Buy time: **두 시간 주세요** (*du sigan juseyo*) — "Two hours, please"
> - Check remaining time: **시간 얼마나 남았어요?** (*sigan eolmana namasseoyo?*) — "How much time do I have left?"
> - Order food: **라면 하나 주세요** (*ramyeon hana juseyo*) — "One bowl of ramyeon, please"

## How do you get a seat at a Korean PC bang?

Say **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*), "Is there a seat available?" The staff will show a floor-plan screen — green means open, red means taken. Point to a number or say **편한 거 주세요** (*pyeonhan geo juseyo*), "Give me a comfortable one."

Then say how long you plan to stay:

| How long | Say this |
|---|---|
| One hour | 한 시간 주세요 (*han sigan juseyo*) |
| Two hours | 두 시간 주세요 (*du sigan juseyo*) |
| Top up mid-session | 시간 추가해 주세요 (*sigan chugahae juseyo*) |
| Pay at the end | 나갈 때 계산할게요 (*nagal ttae gyesanhalgeyo*) |

**시간 추가해 주세요** (*sigan chugahae juseyo*), "Please add more time," is the phrase for when the countdown in the corner of the screen runs low — staff add minutes in seconds without you standing up.

## How do you order food from your seat?

Most PC bangs have an in-seat phone or on-screen menu. To call the desk, say **주문할게요** (*jumunhalgeyo*), "I'd like to order."

You: **라면 하나 주세요.** — *ramyeon hana juseyo.* — "One bowl of ramyeon, please."  
Them: **지금 바로 드릴게요.** — *jigeum baro deurilgeyo.* — "We'll bring it right away."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

Food comes to the seat. Add **수저도 주세요** (*sujeodo juseyo*), "Chopsticks and a spoon too, please," if the tray arrives without cutlery.

## How do you check out when you are done?

Ask **시간 얼마나 남았어요?** (*sigan eolmana namasseoyo?*), "How much time do I have left?" if the timer is hard to find. To add time, say **한 시간 더 주세요** (*han sigan deo juseyo*), "One more hour, please."

When you are done, head to the desk and say **지금 나갈게요** (*jigeum nagalgeyo*), "I'm heading out now." Most PC bangs bill per minute, so odd amounts like ₩3,200 are normal — just wait for the screen to show the total and tap your card.
