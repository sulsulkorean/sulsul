To ask for a taxi in Korean, say **택시 불러주세요** — *taeksi bulreojuseyo*, meaning "Please call a taxi." This phrase is essential for travelers needing a quick ride. You can also use **택시 타고 싶어요** — *taeksi tago sipeoyo*, which means "I want to take a taxi."

> - **택시 불러주세요** — *taeksi bulreojuseyo* means "Please call a taxi."
> - **택시 타고 싶어요** — *taeksi tago sipeoyo* translates to "I want to take a taxi."
> - **어디로 가요?** — *eodiro gayo?* means "Where are you going?"
> - **영수증 주세요** — *yeongsujeung juseyo* asks for a receipt.
> - **강남역으로 가주세요** — *gangnamyeogeuro gajuseyo* means "Please go to Gangnam Station."

## What to Say When You Need a Taxi

When you need to request a taxi, use **택시 타고 싶어요** — *taeksi tago sipeoyo*. This is a polite and effective way to express your desire to take a taxi, whether you're at a hotel, restaurant, or on the street.

## How to Communicate Your Destination

Once you're in the taxi, the driver might ask, **어디로 가요?** — *eodiro gayo?*, meaning "Where are you going?" Respond with your destination followed by **으로 가주세요** — *euro gajuseyo*, "please go to." For example, **강남역으로 가주세요** — *gangnamyeogeuro gajuseyo* means "Please go to Gangnam Station."

You: **강남역으로 가주세요** — *gangnamyeogeuro gajuseyo* — "Please go to Gangnam Station."  
Them: **알겠습니다** — *algetseumnida* — "Understood."  
You: **감사합니다** — *gamsahamnida* — "Thank you."

## What Usually Goes Wrong

A common mistake is forgetting to specify your destination clearly. Always state the place name followed by **으로 가주세요** — *euro gajuseyo*. This ensures the driver knows exactly where to take you. Additionally, some learners hesitate to confirm the fare, which can lead to misunderstandings. Always ask **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* to clarify the cost.

## Useful Taxi Phrases in Different Situations

Here's a table of useful phrases for various taxi-related situations:

| Situation                   | Phrase                                      |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Requesting a taxi           | **택시 타고 싶어요** — *taeksi tago sipeoyo* |
| Confirming the destination  | **이 주소로 가주세요** — *i jusoro gajuseyo* |
| Asking for a receipt        | **영수증 주세요** — *yeongsujeung juseyo*   |
| Asking the fare             | **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?*                |
| Requesting a stop           | **여기서 세워주세요** — *yeogiseo sewojuseyo* |

## Steps to Successfully Request a Taxi

1. Approach a hotel concierge or a local and say **택시 타고 싶어요** — *taeksi tago sipeoyo*.
2. When the taxi arrives, greet the driver with a polite **안녕하세요** — *annyeonghaseyo*.
3. State your destination, or show an address on your phone and say **이 주소로 가주세요** — *i jusoro gajuseyo*, "please go to this address."
4. Confirm the fare with **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?*, meaning "How much is it?"
5. Upon arrival, ask for a receipt with **영수증 주세요** — *yeongsujeung juseyo*.
