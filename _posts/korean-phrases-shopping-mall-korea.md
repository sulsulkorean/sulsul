Korean phrases for shopping at a mall boil down to price, size, and color. Ask **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?" when you pick something up, then **다른 색 있어요?** (*dareun saek isseoyo?*), "Do you have another color?" if the tag shows a shade you do not want.

> - Ask the price: **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?"
> - Try it on: **입어 봐도 돼요?** (*ibeo bwado dwaeyo?*), "Can I try this on?"
> - Another size: **더 큰 사이즈 있어요?** (*deo keun saijeu isseoyo?*), "Do you have a bigger size?"
> - Pay by card: **카드로 할게요** (*kadeuro halgeyo*), "I will pay by card."

## How do you start a fitting-room visit?

Staff in Seoul malls usually speak enough English for numbers, but Korean gets you faster service when the floor is busy.

1. Hold up the item and ask **이거 얼마예요?**
2. If you like it, ask **입어 봐도 돼요?** and follow the gesture toward fitting rooms.
3. Inside, check the mirror — if it is tight, ask **더 큰 사이즈 있어요?**
4. At checkout, listen for the total, then say **카드로 할게요**.
5. Take the receipt; some stores ask you to show it at the exit tag scanner.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Price check | 이거 얼마예요? (*igeo eolmayeyo?*) |
| Try on | 입어 봐도 돼요? (*ibeo bwado dwaeyo?*) |
| Different color | 다른 색 있어요? (*dareun saek isseoyo?*) |
| Too expensive | 좀 비싸네요 (*jom bissaneyo*) |
| Bag please | 봉투 주세요 (*bongtu juseyo*) |

## What does the clerk say when you ask?

Expect short numbers and one follow-up about payment.

You: **이거 얼마예요?** — *igeo eolmayeyo?* — "How much is this?"  
Them: **삼만 원이에요.** — *samman wonieyo.* — "It is thirty thousand won."  
You: **입어 봐도 돼요?** — *ibeo bwado dwaeyo?* — "Can I try this on?"

If they shake their head at a size, they may say **품절이에요** (*pumjeorieyo*), "It is sold out." Ask **다른 색 있어요?** before you give up — the same cut often comes in another color with stock left.

## What usually goes wrong while shopping?

The most common freeze happens at the fitting-room door when you forgot the phrase and mime instead. **입어 봐도 돼요?** is the whole sentence; no grammar table required.

A second mistake is saying **싸게 해 주세요** (*ssage hae juseyo*), "Make it cheaper," in a fixed-price mall store — that line fits open-air markets, not department floors. For market bargaining, see the post on [Korean phrases for bargaining in markets](/korean-phrases-bargaining-markets).
