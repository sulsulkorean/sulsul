To politely ask for help in Korean, say **도와주세요** (*dowajuseyo*), "please help me." It is polite **해요체** (*haeyoche*) and works with any stranger. Follow it with the exact problem — a lost wallet, an address on your phone — so they know what to do.

> - Open with **실례합니다** (*sillyehamnida*), "Excuse me," before interrupting someone.
> - Say **도움이 필요해요** (*doumi piryohaeyo*), "I need help," when you have no time to explain.
> - If the reply is too fast, say **천천히 말씀해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malsseumhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly."
> - For real danger: **경찰을 불러 주세요** (*gyeongchareul bulleo juseyo*), "Please call the police."

## How do you ask a stranger for directions?

Show the place on your phone. Do not try to pronounce a long address from memory.

You: **실례합니다. 이 주소가 어디예요?** — *sillyehamnida. i jusoga eodiyeyo?* — "Excuse me. Where is this address?"  
Them: **여기서 오른쪽으로 가세요.** — *yeogiseo oreunjjogeuro gaseyo.* — "Go right from here."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

## What do you say when you can't follow the answer?

You: **죄송하지만 잘 모르겠어요.** — *joesonghajiman jal moreugesseoyo.* — "I'm sorry, but I don't understand."  
Them: **영어 할 줄 아세요?** — *yeongeo hal jul aseyo?* — "Do you know English?"  
You: **아니요, 다시 한번 말씀해 주세요.** — *aniyo, dasi hanbeon malsseumhae juseyo.* — "No, please say that once more."

**잘 모르겠어요** (*jal moreugesseoyo*) is honest. **다시 한번 말씀해 주세요** (*dasi hanbeon malsseumhae juseyo*) asks for one repeat — not a blank stare.

## What if you lost something?

Name the item first.

| Need | Say this |
|---|---|
| Get attention | 실례합니다 (*sillyehamnida*) |
| Lost wallet | 지갑을 잃어버렸어요 (*jigabeul ireobeoryeosseoyo*) |
| Lost-and-found | 분실물 센터가 어디예요? (*bunsilmul senteoga eodiyeyo?*) |
| Speak slowly | 천천히 말씀해 주세요 (*cheoncheonhi malsseumhae juseyo*) |
| Call police | 경찰을 불러 주세요 (*gyeongchareul bulleo juseyo*) |

A common mistake: using casual **도와줘** (*dowajwo*) with a stranger. Drop the polite ending and it sounds rude. Stick to **도와주세요**.

## Steps when you need help

1. Say **실례합니다**.
2. Say **도와주세요** and name the problem.
3. Show a phone screen if it is about a place or an item.
4. If you miss the reply, ask them to say it again slowly.
5. Thank them with **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*).
