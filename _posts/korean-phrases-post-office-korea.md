Korean phrases for sending a package at the post office come down to one opening line and two follow-ups. Say **택배 보내고 싶어요** (*taekbae bonaego sipeoyo*), "I'd like to send a package," then name the destination country and choose air or surface mail when the clerk asks.

> - Open with **택배 보내고 싶어요** (*taekbae bonaego sipeoyo*), "I'd like to send a package."
> - Name the country: **미국으로 보내요** (*migugeuro bonaeyo*), "I'm sending it to the United States."
> - Ask about speed: **항공편으로 보낼 수 있어요?** (*hanggongpyeoneuro bonael su isseoyo?*), "Can I send it by air?"
> - If you miss a word, say **다시 말해 주세요** (*dasi malhae juseyo*), "Please say that again."

## How do you walk up to the counter?

Korean post offices — **우체국** (*ucheguk*) — usually have a dedicated counter for domestic parcels and another for international mail. Look for **국제 우편** (*gukje upyeon*, international mail) on the sign above the window.

You: **안녕하세요, 택배 보내고 싶어요.** — *annyeonghaseyo, taekbae bonaego sipeoyo.* — "Hello, I'd like to send a package."  
Them: **어디로 보내세요?** — *eodiro bonaeseyo?* — "Where are you sending it?"  
You: **미국으로 보내요.** — *migugeuro bonaeyo.* — "I'm sending it to the United States."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Start at the counter | 택배 보내고 싶어요 (*taekbae bonaego sipeoyo*) |
| Name destination | 미국으로 보내요 (*migugeuro bonaeyo*) |
| Ask about air mail | 항공편으로 보낼 수 있어요? (*hanggongpyeoneuro bonael su isseoyo?*) |
| Need a box | 상자 있어요? (*sangja isseoyo?*) |
| Get tracking number | 송장 번호 알려 주세요 (*songjang beonho allyeo juseyo*) |

## What does the clerk ask after the destination?

Once you name the country, the clerk asks about weight and delivery speed. These replies are short — one phrase each, no full sentences needed.

You: **항공편으로 보낼 수 있어요?** — *hanggongpyeoneuro bonael su isseoyo?* — "Can I send it by air?"  
Them: **네, 가능해요. 무게를 재 볼게요.** — *ne, ganeunghaeyo. muge-reul jae bolgeyo.* — "Yes, that's possible. I'll weigh it."  
You: **네, 감사합니다.** — *ne, gamsahamnida.* — "Yes, thank you."

If you need the package to arrive faster, ask **특급으로 보낼 수 있어요?** (*teukgeubeuro bonael su isseoyo?*), "Can I send it express?" The clerk prints a label and gives you a **송장 번호** (*songjang beonho*, tracking number). If asked about contents, say **옷이에요** (*osieyo*), "It's clothes," or **선물이에요** (*seonmurieyo*), "It's a gift."

## What usually goes wrong before you even arrive?

Travelers often find the post office but cannot locate the international counter. Use the textbook direction pattern **어디 있어요?** (*eodi isseoyo?*) at the entrance.

You: **저기요, 국제 우편 어디 있어요?** — *jeogiyo, gukje upyeon eodi isseoyo?* — "Excuse me, where is international mail?"  
Them: **2층 오른쪽이에요.** — *icheung oreunjjog-ieyo.* — "It's on the second floor, to the right."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

A second mistake is bringing an unpackaged item without asking for a box first. Say **상자 있어요?** (*sangja isseoyo?*), "Do you have a box?" before you start filling out the form.
